📊 Superstore Global E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Dashboard
📌 Executive Summary
This project presents an interactive, executive-level Power BI Sales Analysis Dashboard built on the global Superstore e-commerce dataset. The dashboard consolidates transactional data into actionable business metrics—evaluating top-line revenue growth, regional sales performance, order distributions, and category margins across global markets.

Key Dashboard Highlights & Features:
Global KPI Banner: Instant high-level summary tracking $13M Revenue, $408.51K Total Profit, 11.99% Profit Margin, and 14K Total Orders.

Global Slicer Panel: Top-level interactive filtering allowing dynamic segmentation by Year (2011–2014), Product Category (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology), and Market Region.

Regional Market Distribution: Clear breakdowns comparing order volumes and overall revenue generation across global markets (APAC, US, LATAM, EU, EMEA, Africa, Canada).

Geographic Sales Breakdown: Scrollable visual tracking country-level revenue rankings, identifying key anchor markets led by the United States and Australia.

💡 Key Business Insights
Top-Line Revenue Expansion: Global sales demonstrated consistent multi-year expansion, nearly doubling overall top-line revenue between 2011 and 2014.

Category Margin Dynamics: Technology remains the primary driver of healthy profit margins (~13–14%), whereas Furniture suffers from margin compression down to ~6.4%.

Regional Dominance: The United States and APAC regions consistently generate the highest cumulative sales and order counts, serving as core revenue drivers.

Impact of Loss-Leader Pricing: Deep promotional discounting (> 20%) severely erodes bottom-line margins across all product categories, driving negative returns on heavily discounted items.

🛠️ Strategic Recommendations
Discount Ceiling Strategy: Cap maximum promotional discounts at 20% to prevent severe profit margin erosion on high-volume product lines.

Furniture Operations Optimization: Renegotiate supplier unit costs and freight expenses for bulky goods to lift Furniture margins closer to the organizational baseline (~12%).

Targeted Customer Loyalty: Focus promotional and marketing budgets on high-value repeat buyers in key anchor markets (US, Australia, France).
